What would you do next?

You are helping a research team prepare work for a colleague. Keep the original files and write down what you changed. This lets a colleague check your work without guessing. Choose a project below, then follow the work from the researcher to the people who need to check it.

A data steward is a colleague who helps organise and check research material. A privacy reviewer checks how people could be affected. An archive curator helps decide whether an archive can look after the files. You will take these roles in turn, with a question to answer at each handoff.

              After the handoff: what could make this work easier?
              Look back at where someone needed to ask a question or return your work. Was the problem an unclear request, a missing file, or a decision only another person could make?
              A shared checklist might help the researcher remember the method note. A program might compare a file list with the files or remind a reviewer about an unanswered request. These are ideas to investigate, not capabilities implemented in this exercise. Try a small change with the people doing the work and check whether it prevents mistakes. Time savings have not been measured.
              This page already collects your selected answers, assigns example reference numbers and moves between practice roles. It does not inspect files or decide consent. A better form may help someone prepare a request; it cannot take over a community decision or remove the need for a specialist review.
