# Standards and source register

These sources inform a **design**, not a claim that the prototype implements or certifies conformance. Adoption, jurisdiction, actual policy wording and current applicability must be confirmed with relevant owners in a pilot.

- [RDA DMP Common Standard WG](https://www.rd-alliance.org/groups/dmp-common-standards-wg/outputs/?output=94576): a structured, machine-actionable DMP exchange direction. We propose a **later mapping/export**, not a claim of current compliance or a mandated authoring form.
- [W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/): entities, activities and agents underpin an interoperable model for provenance. A UI can ask for inputs/outputs, activity, actor and time without requiring researchers to write RDF.
- [PREMIS preservation metadata](https://www.loc.gov/standards/premis/): informs preservation events, objects, agents and rights evidence. A checklist or checksum alone is not a PREMIS implementation.
- [FAIR principles](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/): findability and accessibility do not demand universally open data. Access may be conditional; inappropriate public metadata can still be harmful.
- [Global Indigenous Data Alliance CARE principles](https://www.gida-global.org/careprinciples): Collective Benefit, Authority to Control, Responsibility and Ethics inform questions about governance and affected communities, not a generic AI-generated community policy.
- [UNESCO Recommendation on Open Science](https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-open-science): supports inclusive approaches and the recognition of diverse knowledge systems; does not substitute for actual agreements.
- [CoreTrustSeal requirements and certification](https://www.coretrustseal.org/): repository trustworthiness can inform handoff due diligence; certification is not a dataset-specific retention or access guarantee.
- [EUR-Lex GDPR Regulation 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng): where EU personal-data law applies, processing and research safeguards require contextual legal assessment. This project gives **no lawful-basis determination** and does not infer that pseudonymised data are freely shareable.
- [DataCite Metadata Schema 4.7](https://schema.datacite.org/meta/kernel-4.7/), [RO-Crate](https://www.researchobject.org/ro-crate/specification), and [BagIt RFC 8493](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc8493/): candidate mappings for identifying/citing related research objects and packaging transfer manifests; the prototype generates none of these.
- [FITS](https://fits.gsfc.nasa.gov/fits_standard.html), [DDI Lifecycle](https://ddialliance.org/ddi-lifecycle), [GA4GH Data Use Ontology](https://www.ga4gh.org/product/data-use-ontology-duo/), [Darwin Core](https://dwc.tdwg.org/), and [BIDS](https://bids-specification.readthedocs.io/en/stable/): discipline-specific targets used in the fictional evaluation. No format, controlled-vocabulary, consent or domain-schema conformance is asserted.

### Evidence and limits

These are standards, normative principles and official texts, not primary research measuring the proposed tool's effectiveness. User need and workflow assumptions in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) remain **hypotheses to test**. Institution-specific and disciplinary rules, consent documents and community protocols are intentionally *not* fabricated here. Real rule releases require source, issuer, version, curator approval and effective interval; stale or ambiguous source material must produce a referral rather than an automatic decision.
